01 / FAMILY & MATRIMONIAL

Family & matrimonial disputes

Mr Ma combines a detailed understanding of decision-making in Mainland Chinese courts with experience of overseas legal practice. Working with Dacheng offices and senior lawyers in the United Kingdom, Germany, Canada, Australia, Singapore, Hong Kong and other jurisdictions, he has acted in complex and high-value divorce and succession disputes.

Principal legal issues

  • Global matrimonial-asset due diligence, valuation and legal analysis
  • Forum selection, parallel proceedings and conflicts of laws between jurisdictions
  • Jurisdiction following a Chinese citizen’s acquisition of foreign nationality, and recognition and enforcement of foreign judgments
  • Validity of cross-border pre-nuptial and post-nuptial agreements, nominee ownership, asset transfers and division of equity interests
  • Status and scope of heirs, authenticity and validity of wills, and applicable law in cross-border succession

02 / SUCCESSION & CORPORATE CONTROL

Succession & corporate control

Mr Ma has acted in a number of related commercial disputes following the unexpected death of a company controller, including contests among family members and between family members and other shareholders for control of the company. Some related cases have been decided by the Supreme People’s Court.

Principal legal issues

  • Control of business licences, accounting records and company chops, and authority to represent the company’s will
  • Validity of shareholder and board resolutions following the death of a chair
  • Damage to company or shareholder interests following the death of a major shareholder or controller
  • Control disputes among shareholders of offshore holding companies with operating entities in Mainland China
  • Tax, securities-regulatory, foreign-exchange and criminal issues—including bigamy and occupational embezzlement—arising from family conflict
