BACKGROUND & APPROACH

Professional background

Naidong Ma focuses on matrimonial and family disputes involving high-net-worth individuals, cross-border succession, equity succession and private wealth management. In complex disputes, he considers family relationships and corporate governance together.

His experience includes parallel proceedings in China and abroad, listed-company control issues arising from matrimonial changes, matters spanning civil, commercial and criminal law, and multi-jurisdictional pre-marital property and will planning.

In cross-border matters, he works with local counsel and provides expert opinions on Chinese law for overseas proceedings. He works in Chinese and English.
